## 2. The money split (15 min — the important one)

This is the conversation nobody wants to have and everybody regrets skipping. Have it now while there's no money to fight over.

Answer these out loud, together:
- When a placement fee comes in, how is it split? (equal thirds? by who sourced vs who closed?)
- If one person does most of the work one month, does the split change or stay fixed?
- Money in the business (a domain, ads, tools) — who pays, out of what?
- If someone leaves in 3 months, what do they keep?

You don't need a lawyer. You need one honest WhatsApp message in the group that all three thumbs-up. That message is your protection.

Decision to write down: split = ______

---

## 3. Who owns what (10 min)

Three people with no lanes means everything gets done twice or not at all. Give each person ONE clear job.
- **Candidate side** — sourcing, screening, getting CVs ready. (who: ____)
- **Client side** — finding companies, reaching HR, the half that pays. (who: ____)
- **Coordination** — the tracker, follow-ups, keeping it moving. (who: ____)

One person can own more than one, but every job has exactly one owner.

Decision to write down: owners = ______

Not more candidates. **One company with one real opening.**
