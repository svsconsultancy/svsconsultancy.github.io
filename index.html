Common questions

Straight answers to what people usually ask.

What kind of roles do you work on?

Freshers, career switchers, people restarting after a break, BPO roles, and MNC roles. If you're not sure whether we can help, just send your CV and we'll tell you honestly.

How do you work with employers?

We agree terms with you in writing first, then source and screen candidates against your actual requirement. Every placement comes with a replacement guarantee if a candidate leaves early.

How fast do you send candidates?

We screen for skills and communication before you see anyone, then send a shortlist quickly so roles don't stay open. Timing depends on the role, and we'll be upfront about it.

How do I get started?

Email us your CV (if you're job hunting) or your hiring requirement (if you're an employer) and we'll get back to you.
