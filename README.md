# SVS Consultancy — Operations Console

A simple, one-file web app to run a recruitment / placement consultancy. No install, no login, no server. Open the link, start working.

**Live app:** _(GitHub Pages link appears here after setup)_

## What it does

- **Dashboard** — your business at a glance, with the one number that matters (open client requirements)
- **Candidates** — track your candidate pipeline from new to placed
- **Clients & Outreach** — track companies and HR contacts (the side that pays)
- **Submission Log** — dated proof of every candidate you send to a company (protects your fee)
- **Messages** — ready-to-copy outreach for candidates, employers, and terms confirmation
- **HR Playbook** — how to find HR, talk money, and avoid getting cheated
- **Fee Calculator** — quote clients and plan monthly targets
- **90-Day Plan** — the launch order that actually works, with tick-off progress
- **Backup** — export/import your data as a file

## How your data is stored

Everything you type is saved **in your own browser, on your own device**. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. The public app ships empty — visitors see a blank tool, never your data.

Because data lives in the browser:
- Use the same browser on the same device to keep your data
- **Back up weekly** (Backup tab) so you don't lose it
- To move to a new device, export on the old one and import on the new one

## For partners

Each person opens the same live link and keeps their own data. To share one master list, have one person own it, export weekly, and send the backup file to the others.

## Run it locally instead

Download `index.html` and double-click it. Works fully offline.
